# From hallway walk to shareable map

**Release outcome.** An organizer starts a walk offline, sees which side of a turn needs another view, marks a doorway, and finishes on another floor. Their iPhone keeps the walk, uploads it when permitted, and opens a private result in **Your Maps**. After the owner chooses **Anyone with the link**, a visitor opens it in Safari, identifies the entrance and level change, and follows the recorded path. The Mac remains available for comparison and export.

**Proposed owners.** Anish leads the native scanner and fusion, Krishna partners on keyframes, checkpoints and field validation, Sam owns auth, ingestion, workers and access, and Arielle owns map management and the guest experience. Anish and Arielle design the scanner and viewer together. These are proposed work assignments. [Team roadmap](ROADMAP.md) · [Capture trial](https://github.com/darkprince558/path-app/issues/4) · [Second-phone trial](https://github.com/darkprince558/path-app/issues/5)

[Try the interactive design preview](hallway-experience.html). It uses invented geometry and simulated network and sensor events. The first exercise is to reobserve a corner, mark a doorway, finish offline, reconnect and open the visitor map.

## What people see

| Moment | Primary interaction | What happens when something goes wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First launch | **Start walk** and **Saved walks**. Ask for the camera when starting. Offer a short practice corner with a skip action. Show available storage and whether this device supports LiDAR. Sign-in can wait until upload. | A visitor on an unsupported phone can open maps. A recorder with little space sees how much space to free and can still open saved work. |
| Recording | The camera stays visible. A thumb-reachable mini-map shows the current floor, camera direction and walked path. Tap to expand. **Mark doorway** gives immediate feedback and Undo. **Finish** opens a review. | Show one actionable message at a time. “Hold still while tracking recovers” takes precedence over “Look back at the left doorway.” Keep the last saved section visible. |
| Level change | Show **Between levels** while climbing. On a stable landing, suggest a level, with a one-tap correction. Allow floor names such as Ground, Mezzanine and Upper. | Stairs, ramps and lifts can be tagged. When the intervening path has no usable tracking, display a gap. Let the owner confirm the transition type. |
| Finish and review | Lead with a floor plan, duration, distance, and the two most useful repairs. Tapping a weak corner focuses that place. Offer **Continue scanning** or **Save walk**. | A user can keep a partial walk. Show the last saved section and any missing range. Continuing after an AR session reset requires a verified match or creates a separate segment. |
| Saved walks and uploads | Give each walk a name and date, a thumbnail, floor count, and a plain-language state. Allow rename, retry, Wi-Fi-only and explicit cellular upload. Show acknowledged bytes and sections. | **Saved on this phone**, **Waiting for Wi-Fi**, **Sign in to upload** and **Processing failed** each have one relevant action. The user can leave the screen and come back. |
| Guest on a phone | Open the floor plan first with **Start**, **Finish**, marked doors and the level transition. Offer **3D**, **Fit map**, a floor selector and an ordered text list of landmarks. A visitor can use the link without installing Path Capture. | A slow connection or WebGL failure leaves the lightweight floor plan and landmark list usable. The owner chooses whether quality warnings are included in a guest view. |

**Simplify the screens people already use.** The scanner already has Start walk, tracking status, saved-section counts and a saved-scan list. The individual scan review also exposes Mac pairing, native publication, LiDAR bundle preparation and a complete archive. Group sections under their walk and make **Open map** or **Upload walk** the primary action for that state. Place Mac export, file inventory, device IDs and diagnostics under **Export and details**. Keep the visible evidence of a successful local save. On the guest screen, remember the selected floor when switching views and give every destination a shareable name the owner has reviewed.

**Visual direction.** Use the existing cream and forest surfaces for the library and review, the dark canvas for spatial views, and the coral ring for the current position and recorded path. Show single-view coverage with hatching, repeat observations with a solid fill, and unobserved space with a neutral background. Use a distinct symbol for tracking loss. Keep data labels short and attach prompts to the relevant map location. Default to a fixed map orientation and a rotating camera arrow. **Follow me** recenters after manual exploration. [Existing design system](../apps/web/DESIGN.md)

**Accessibility and walking ergonomics.** Use large thumb targets, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver names and status summaries, redundant shapes and labels, and reduced-motion behavior. Make haptics and spoken cues optional. Announce meaningful state changes once. Test glare, low light, one-handed use, left-handed reach, large text and silent mode. Delay nonurgent rescan prompts on stairs until the next landing. Keep capture usable with the map collapsed. Provide an explicit action for every gesture, including recentering and changing level.

## A. Shared format, identities and sign-in

- Define a versioned raw walk envelope alongside the current mesh-bundle v1. Introduce a stable device walk UUID, section UUID and sequence number, coordinate-frame UUID, monotonic walk-relative timestamps and UTC creation time. Store each section’s time origin explicitly. Retries preserve those IDs.
- Record metric, right-handed, Y-up coordinates, column-major camera-to-walk transforms, tracking states, RGB and depth dimensions, depth encoding/scale, confidence encoding, calibration, and camera convention. Store the intrinsics for the actual saved image resolution or an explicit conversion. For the first producer use unrotated JPEG, Float32 little-endian depth in metres, matching UInt8 confidence and calibrated camera dimensions. The manifest declares dimensions, timestamps, encoding and registration for every stream. Include a depth-to-camera transform if the producer supplies unregistered depth. Test pixel deprojection, the camera forward-axis conversion and translation against an asymmetric fixture at a known position.
- Store marker observations as separate records with stable landmark ID, floor, section, timestamp, frame ID, and a measured 3D point or a clearly labelled camera-only observation. Include provenance and schema version for derived floor/coverage data. Canonicalize float roundoff in the homogeneous row within a tested tolerance and reject genuinely non-affine matrices.
- Agree a fixture before splitting implementation. The fixture starts with `walk W / section S1 / frame F1 / lower / doorway D1`, then `S2 / F7 / upper` at the same X/Z, then `S3 / F12 / lower / D1`. Give it known geometry, a section time reset, one tracking gap, duplicated upload attempts and a deliberately incorrect return match. Check that both floors and both D1 observations survive round trips.
- Register the exact production web callback `https://not404.vodka/auth/callback` and intended logout return URL in the tenant. Verify the full Safari login with a real account. Register a separate Auth0 Native app, use Authorization Code with PKCE through the system browser, store credentials in Keychain, and call the existing API bearer-token path. Associate queued uploads with the verified account and space. After an account switch, offer an explicit import choice. [Auth0 native flow](https://auth0.com/docs/get-started/authentication-and-authorization-flow/authorization-code-flow-with-pkce)

## B. Native capture, recovery and accuracy

1. **Durable capture.** Extend `CaptureController`, `WalkMap`, `WalkMapView` and the walk exporter from PR 39. Journal committed sections atomically and save interruption reason, free space, thermal state, dropped depth frames and writing backlog. On relaunch, reconcile the journal with verified section files. Preserve complete sections and identify the incomplete tail. Foreground recording and background upload have distinct lifecycle behavior.
2. **Coverage evidence.** Keep 25 cm plan cells initially. Key evidence by coordinate frame, level, cell and surface-height band. Accumulate bounded observations from good tracked depth, confidence, viewpoint direction and distinct viewpoints. A floor hit cannot fill in a doorway wall. Tag feature-only evidence separately. Deduplicate rollover frames and repeated samples from a stationary phone.
3. **Keyframe selection and coaching.** Save extra calibrated RGB-D frames when heading changes around a corner or a doorway marker is placed. Bound by file-writing capacity and device thermal state. Use a replay-tuned minimum translation/angle change to recognize distinct views. Suppress prompts during tracking loss, apply hysteresis and a cooldown, and clear a prompt only after new evidence. Compare the new policy against the current policy on the same frames, then measure it prospectively.
4. **Floors.** Combine gravity-aligned heights with nearby floor-plane evidence, a stable landing window and manual correction. Keep a transition segment through stairs or ramps. Associate derived points and path segments to the same level IDs. Record original heights so a mistaken floor assignment can be corrected without recapture.
5. **Doorway checks.** Ask the user to aim at the same identifiable jamb or tagged point on departure and return. Save the hit and its uncertainty separately from the phone pose. Reject ambiguous matches between similar doors. Report horizontal and vertical separation, sample support and, where a reference orientation was actually observed, heading difference. An open-ended walk gets an explicit “No return observation” result.
6. **Resume and repair.** Within an uninterrupted tracked session, continue the existing frame. After a reset, try relocalization at a saved doorway and verify against overlapping local geometry. Until verified, preserve the new segment in its own frame. A manual revisit or later rescan creates new evidence with lineage. Owners can compare revisions. Retain original poses and surface measurements if a later optimization proposes corrections. [Apple’s relocalization example](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/arkit/saving-and-loading-world-data)

## C. Resumable data transfer and Vultr processing

**Upload contract.** Proposed routes below describe behavior for a finished offline walk. Start sending after **Finish** for the first release. Uploading sealed sections during a walk can reuse the protocol after profiling capture contention.

| Client action | Proposed API and persistence |
| --- | --- |
| Register | `POST /api/walks` with owner space, stable walk ID, sealed inventory digest, ordered sections and part digests/sizes. Use one capture row keyed by owner + device walk ID and a discriminated raw-walk manifest. Identical registration returns the same server ID. Changed content under that ID returns a conflict. |
| Recover progress | `GET /api/walks/:id/upload-status` returns missing or acknowledged part IDs and bytes to the owner. New tables record section/part receipts, size and digest. Server receipts resolve disagreements after a crash. |
| Transfer | `PUT /api/walks/:id/sections/:sectionId/parts/:part` accepts an assigned, bounded part. Stream to private staging, verify exact size and SHA-256, atomically promote, then acknowledge. Duplicate identical bytes are harmless. Concurrent differing bytes fail without replacing the first. |
| Finalize | `POST /api/walks/:id/finalize` verifies the ordered manifest, complete inventory and ownership, then binds one immutable input digest to one queued job transactionally. Missing/corrupt parts are identified for retry. Reuse `GET /api/jobs/:id` for worker progress. |
| Review and publish | Return an owner-accessible draft result, quality report and revision ID. Use the existing approval path for a private map. Then the owner can choose guest visibility. Repeated approval resolves to the same scan and link. |

- **Phone.** Persist immutable part files and a small upload ledger. Use one background URLSession with file-backed tasks, modest concurrency and queued-byte progress. Reassociate system tasks on relaunch. Reconcile receipts after a force-quit or network change and renew expired credentials before retry. Offer Wi-Fi preference and pause/cancel for transfers. Keep the local walk available until server verification and the owner’s retention choice. [Apple background transfer limits](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/downloading-files-in-the-background)
- **Transfer size.** The existing v1 limit is 32 MiB/file and 256 MiB/bundle. Preserve those validations. For raw walks, start with 8 MiB part files and an explicit per-walk quota. Benchmark an allowlist of poses, calibrated RGB, depth, confidence, markers and level evidence. Exclude MP4, redundant section meshes and full-rate motion from the standard upload unless the selected processor demonstrably needs them. Enforce decompressed sizes, dimensions and per-account storage quotas before decode. Use a private durable volume for the pilot, with capacity alerts, cleanup of abandoned staging, and a tested restore.
- **Worker.** Introduce a pinned raw RGB-D processor identity. Reuse job ownership, leases, heartbeat, CPU exclusion and immutable revision promotion. Port the PR 39 Open3D TSDF implementation to the Linux worker with pinned versions, cancellation, a memory ceiling and fixture parity against the Mac. Compute projection using per-frame intrinsics and recorded poses. Split at unresolved tracking breaks and fuse verified sections in one walk frame. Stream sections and use bounded spatial subvolumes for long walks, preserving cross-boundary overlap and global coordinates.
- **Outputs.** Produce a metric master, mobile GLB, preview, floor plan, simplified walked polylines with breaks, level transitions, approved marker candidates and a versioned QA report. Reuse the existing mesh cleanup/mobile optimizer where compatible. Add a raw-walk provenance adapter and explicit output allowlist and size limits. The present CPU adapter expects a v1 input, specific files and its own report digest. Approval independently validates input digest, processor version, coordinate frame and output hashes before publishing. Keep raw sign-in credentials and source imagery out of guest derivatives.
- **Failure behavior.** Missing depth, an unresolved alignment, a failed job and an incomplete upload get distinct states. A recoverable worker crash reuses the immutable input and existing fencing. A corrected algorithm or explicit reprocess creates a new versioned attempt/revision with provenance. Keep the last approved map available while its replacement is a draft. Show queue, fusion and optimization stages from actual worker progress.

## D. Sharing, visitor maps and service operation

- **Your Maps** should show thumbnails, names, floor counts and job states. Put incomplete uploads and items requiring review first. Deep-link from an upload notification or the capture library to the same walk. Add search by map or landmark once users have multiple walks.
- **Open quickly.** Load small floor outlines, route and landmarks first. Fetch the mobile GLB on demand when a visitor chooses 3D. Use the same coordinate transform exactly once for mesh, floor bands, doorway pins and route. Preserve selected level and landmark when switching between plan, cutaway and orbit. Provide Fit map, a screen-reader-friendly landmark list and a persistent floor selector on mobile.
- **Separate measured and reviewed information.** A recorded path can contain detours, stairs and tracking gaps. Label it **Walked path**. A visitor-facing navigable route needs independently reviewed connectivity, floor transitions and destination labels. Report an unknown step-free route when the relevant transitions have no verified accessibility data. AI-generated landmark cues go through the existing owner review flow after route verification.
- **Implement the three sharing modes on the real API.** Default a new walk and approved scan to private. Introduce a scoped unlisted grant for an approved scan and its selected derived assets, with a high-entropy token, rotation and revocation. Represent unlisted eligibility explicitly at the space level. One deliberate share action may enable both on a dedicated single-walk space. In a shared multi-scan space, show the current parent policy and require the space owner’s explicit choice before changing it. A private parent blocks anonymous reads. A public scan requires a public parent. An unlisted link grants access only to the selected derivatives of that approved scan. Space listings, sibling scans, private review annotations and raw photos retain their own access checks. Inspect any enum migration shared with events/paths so those resource policies keep their existing meanings.
- **Enforce access on every derivative.** Metadata, GLB, point cloud, floor plan, path, pins, thumbnails and download routes all check current approval, parent policy and the viewer’s grant. Use existing private/no-store proxy reads where revocation is required. Test signed-out, invited, owner and revoked reads by direct asset URL. An already downloaded copy can be kept by its recipient. Keep the previously issued encrypted preview as a separate compatibility case with its own removal procedure.
- **Operate it.** Check in reviewed deployment instructions for pinned API and worker releases, private storage, environment variable names, Auth0 callbacks and rollback. Use readiness checks for upstream API and database, a separate worker heartbeat and disk-capacity checks. Monitor stalled uploads, queue age, processing time, peak worker memory, published asset size and mobile first interaction. Use counts and timings. Keep private map geometry and tokenized links out of telemetry. Rotate previously shared credentials with the service owner and test after rotation.

## E. Field trials and release gates

These are **proposed pilot targets**, to be revisited after the first measured baseline. Record failures and the denominator. Keep raw recordings and venue images local or in approved private storage. Use only synthetic examples in Git.

| Question | Test and initial gate |
| --- | --- |
| Does it keep recording? | A 30-minute foreground walk with at least three level changes, then separate interruption, low-storage and relaunch trials. Zero silent stops or lost committed sections. Every interruption shows its reason, last saved section and recovery action. Record memory, free bytes, temperature state and capture/frame-write latency over time. |
| Is guidance useful? | Replay the original walk to compare algorithms on identical inputs. Then repeat that hallway and walk a different one. Prelabel decision points independently, use the same overlap rule across runs, and aim for bidirectional usable depth at ≥80% of field-labelled turns on each route. Report prompt count, accepted prompts and unnecessary prompts. Investigate the 2:26 case even if the average improves. |
| Is geometry accurate? | Use a marked start/finish point, two marked door jambs, two tape-measured widths and a known floor transition. Repeat at normal walking speed in both directions. Initial targets are ≤30 cm horizontal return discrepancy on a roughly 100 m closed walk, ≤20 cm vertical error on the same landing and ≤15 cm width error. Report median, worst case and number of observations, including misses. Report heading only where the target supplies a direction. Keep projected depth residuals separate from these ground-truth measures. |
| Can it survive bad connectivity? | Start offline, cut Wi-Fi mid-part, change network, expire the access token, force-quit/reopen and retry finalize concurrently. The resulting map contains every acknowledged section exactly once. Missing data has an actionable retry. A second account cannot resume the first account’s upload. |
| Can Vultr handle a long walk? | Replay the synthetic fixture on every relevant change and the approved real walk privately before release. Compare owner frame inventory, bounds, floor transitions and mesh orientation to Mac results for the same fusion settings. On a production-sized staging VM, measure wall time, peak RSS, scratch disk and queue delay for the reference and 30-minute walks. Kill and restart one worker mid-job. Require one complete revision, bounded memory and no partial visible output. |
| Can a visitor use it? | Have five first-time users try a reviewed map without coaching. Aim for four to identify the correct entrance and floor change within 30 seconds, and four to complete recording → review → share without help. Test a real signed-out iPhone over cellular. Target a usable 2D plan in ≤3 s on a documented 10 Mbps profile and record 3D load separately. Repeat with large text, VoiceOver, reduced motion, WebGL disabled and weak network. |
| Is sharing correct after deployment? | Run `make check`, the API auth/processing/approval suite on disposable Postgres, the processor fixture, native fixture/replay checks and web browser tests. On staging exercise public, unlisted and private reads, link rotation, revocation, downgrade of parent visibility, and API/worker restarts. Reopen the same approved link on a second physical phone. |

**Rollout.** First use the fixtures in CI, then a team-only release on device, then a second organizer and independent guest. Gate raw-walk intake, new coverage and guest overlays separately. During rollback stop admitting new-format jobs, drain or preserve queued inputs, keep previously supported readers available and point shares back to their last approved revision. Deploy additive migrations and a worker that can claim both the legacy and new processor identities before enabling any raw-walk writer. Keep the dual reader running while new-format jobs are outstanding. Verify a private-storage backup restore against the saved hashes.
