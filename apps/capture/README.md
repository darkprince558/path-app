# Capture and processing

Krishna and Anish own the phone capture, export and alignment pipeline. Record overlapping views of a recognizable corner with an ordinary phone camera. Use the [offline website product plan](../../planning/offline-web-product.md) to reconstruct a colored PLY and give Arielle and Sam its encoding, size, axes, frame IDs and measured scale reference. Keep local captures in the ignored `captures-local/` directory at the repository root.

Save several camera frames while recording and label which turn or landmark they show. These are the inputs for Gemini’s directions experiment. For the venue milestone, verify scan-to-event alignment before exporting the routing graph in meters.
